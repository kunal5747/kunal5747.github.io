Land acquisition

Status

  • 83 villages notified across Khed, Haveli, Purandar, Bhor, Mulshi, Maval.
  • Sept 2024: West ~90%, East ~70% acquired.
  • 2025–26: West reported 99%+; East still open, Purandar/Bhor (E5–E7) is the lagging block.
  • ~₹11,000 cr compensation outlay.

Compensation route

  • Largely direct purchase / consent award at a multiple of ready reckoner, village-dependent.
  • Incentive for landowners consenting under the direct-purchase policy.
  • Inside ROW = price fixed by the state. Influence belt = market-priced. Financially opposite trades.

Plot-level due diligence

  1. Match village + Gat/Survey no. against the MSRDC notification and the 90–110 m ROW band.
  2. Pull the 7/12 — look for Bhoosampadan / MSRDC / "sampadit" remarks and other-rights entries.
  3. Run a 42-year IGR eSearch (Index II) on the Gat no. — sales, mortgages, PoA chains.
  4. Establish inside-ROW vs influence-belt. This single call decides the whole thesis.
  5. Check PMRDA DP / Regional Plan zoning, green belt, NA conversion, interchange-adjacent reservations.
  6. For E5–E7 (Purandar/Bhor): award and construction pending — add 2–4 years of timeline risk.

# 05 · Hiring & Team

ATLAS is planned as a company that scales over decades. The team is built to
match a philosophy where **humans set direction and approve decisions while AI
agents do the coordination and execution**.

---

## Organizing principle

The human is the CEO; ATLAS is the management team. Internally, that means we
hire people who are excellent at:

- **Judgment** — deciding *what* should be built and *whether* to approve an
  action.
- **Systems thinking** — designing reusable platforms, not one-off features.
- **Domain depth** — especially in the first vertical, construction and real
  estate.

We deliberately keep human headcount lean by leaning on ATLAS itself for
coordination and execution work.

---

## Core functions to staff

| Function | Why it matters early |
|----------|----------------------|
| **Platform engineering** | Owns the reusable AI Agent Platform — the moat. |
| **AI / agent engineering** | Builds and tunes the specialized agents and orchestration. |
| **Vertical (ConstructionOS)** | Domain experts who shape the first product and win early customers. |
| **Security & compliance** | Makes the enterprise-grade promises real and auditable. |
| **Implementation / customer success** | Onboards enterprise customers and drives adoption. |
| **Go-to-market** | Sales, partnerships, and marketing for the first vertical. |

---

## Culture

- **Plan before building.** The same principle we apply in product, we apply in
  how we run the company.
- **Outcome-focused.** People are measured by results delivered, not activity.
- **Human accountability for AI actions.** Every agent operates under a human
  owner; automation never removes responsibility.
- **Replaceable by design.** Just as we keep software components replaceable, we
  document and systematize knowledge so the company is not fragile to any single
  person.

---

## How the team scales with the roadmap

- **Phase 1 — Platform.** A small, senior team focused on the reusable core.
- **Phase 2 — ConstructionOS.** Add construction domain experts and
  implementation/customer-success capacity.
- **Phase 3 — More verticals.** Each new industry OS is seeded by a small
  vertical pod reusing the shared platform, rather than a whole new company.

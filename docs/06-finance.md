# 06 · Finance

The business is designed to build a durable, reusable asset (the platform) and
monetize it repeatedly across industries.

---

## Business strategy

The commercial approach, in order:

1. **Build the reusable AI Agent Platform.**
2. **Launch ConstructionOS** as the first vertical.
3. **Reuse the platform** for additional industries.
4. **Grow recurring enterprise revenue** through subscriptions, implementation
   services, and premium AI capabilities.

The key financial insight: the expensive, hard-to-build asset (the platform) is
built **once** and monetized **many times** as new verticals launch on top of
it.

---

## Revenue model

Three complementary streams:

| Stream | What it is | Why it matters |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| **Subscriptions** | Recurring platform / vertical-OS licenses | Predictable, compounding recurring revenue |
| **Implementation services** | Onboarding, integration, configuration | Wins enterprise deals; funds customer success |
| **Premium AI capabilities** | Advanced agents, higher usage tiers, specialized models | Expands revenue per account over time |

---

## Unit economics logic

- Each new **vertical** reuses the shared platform, so the marginal cost of
  launching an industry OS falls over time.
- Each new **customer** in an existing vertical is largely subscription +
  implementation, with the platform cost already amortized.
- **Premium AI** and expanded usage grow revenue per account without a
  proportional rise in fixed cost.

This is what turns ATLAS from a project into a company that can scale over
decades: a reusable core plus repeatable, recurring monetization.

## Funding and investment

The [Master Blueprint](../README.md) is explicitly intended to serve as a
reference manual for **investors** as well as engineers and executives. Finance
decisions themselves — as with all consequential business actions — sit behind
the [human-in-the-loop](01-product-vision.md#human-in-the-loop) approval model:

> Investments, loans, and bank transfers always require explicit human approval.

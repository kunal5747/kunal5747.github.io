# 07 · Go-to-Market

The strategy avoids building many unrelated products. Instead: **one platform,
launched into the market through industry-specific operating systems**, starting
with the vertical where we have the strongest wedge.

---

## Industry strategy

Rather than many unrelated products, ATLAS is **one platform with
industry-specific operating systems** layered on top. Each vertical shares the
same underlying platform.

Examples of the vertical family:

- ConstructionOS
- HospitalOS
- ManufacturingOS
- LogisticsOS
- FinanceOS
- RetailOS
- AgricultureOS
- HotelOS

Each shares the same [reusable platform](02-architecture.md) — so every vertical
we launch also strengthens the shared core.

---

## First commercial product: ConstructionOS

The first product is **ConstructionOS**, chosen because of existing domain
experience in construction and real estate. This gives a **realistic route to
early customers** while strengthening the shared platform.

Full module detail is in [09 · ConstructionOS](09-constructionos.md).

---

## Why lead with one vertical

- **Credibility.** Domain depth earns trust with enterprise buyers faster than a
  generic "AI platform" pitch.
- **Concrete outcomes.** A vertical sells *results* (faster due diligence,
  tighter cost control), which fits the outcome-focused philosophy.
- **Platform proof.** Delivering a real vertical validates and hardens the
  reusable platform before we scale to more industries.

---

## Motion

1. **Land** early ConstructionOS customers with high-value modules and
   implementation support.
2. **Expand** within each account by activating more modules and premium AI
   capabilities.
3. **Repeat** by launching the next vertical on the same platform, seeded by a
   small vertical pod.

Revenue mechanics for this motion are in [06 · Finance](06-finance.md); timing
is in [08 · Roadmap](08-roadmap.md).

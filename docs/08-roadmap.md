# 08 · Roadmap

ATLAS is planned not as a software project but as a **company that can scale over
decades**. The roadmap moves from a reusable core, to a first vertical, to a
family of industry operating systems.

---

## Phased plan

### Phase 1 — Build the reusable AI Agent Platform

Stand up the durable core: the
[AI Agent Framework](02-architecture.md#ai-agent-framework), the
[Multi-Agent Workflow Engine](02-architecture.md#multi-agent-workflow-engine),
the [Memory System](02-architecture.md#memory-system), the
[Workflow Orchestrator](02-architecture.md#workflow-orchestrator), the
integrations layer, and enterprise controls (permissions, audit, monitoring).

**Goal:** a modular, model-agnostic platform that verticals can be built on.

### Phase 2 — Launch ConstructionOS

Build the first vertical on the platform, targeting a realistic route to early
customers using existing construction/real-estate domain experience.

**Goal:** paying customers, a hardened platform, and proof the reuse model
works. Modules in [09 · ConstructionOS](09-constructionos.md).

### Phase 3 — Reuse the platform for additional industries

Launch further industry operating systems — HospitalOS, ManufacturingOS,
LogisticsOS, FinanceOS, RetailOS, AgricultureOS, HotelOS — each seeded by a
small vertical pod on the shared platform.

**Goal:** a family of verticals, each cheaper to launch than the last.

### Phase 4 — Grow recurring enterprise revenue

Scale [subscriptions, implementation services, and premium AI
capabilities](06-finance.md#revenue-model) across verticals and accounts.

**Goal:** durable, compounding recurring revenue.

## Long-term company vision

ATLAS is intended to evolve from a single product into an **AI company
platform**. The long-term ambition is to become the **digital operating system
that businesses use to coordinate people, software, data, and AI agents**.

```
Platform  →  ConstructionOS  →  Vertical family  →  AI company platform
 (Phase 1)     (Phase 2)          (Phase 3)            (long term)
```

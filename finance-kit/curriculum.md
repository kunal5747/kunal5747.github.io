# 12-Week Hands-On Finance Curriculum

Rule of this curriculum: **every week produces a real-world artifact** —
a document, a meeting, a bank application, a filled template. Reading
without doing doesn't count. Claude (the finance coach) teaches the
concept, you execute in the real world, then report back for review.

Progress is tracked in `journal/progress.md`.

## Phase 1 — Foundation (know your own numbers)

**Week 1 — The deal autopsy.** Concept: margins, breakeven, basis (carpet
vs saleable), all-in vs base pricing. *Action:* fill `jv-deal-model.html`
with FINAL real numbers for the live deal (actual loading %, actual price
list, actual velocity). Deliverable: the corrected model + 5 numbers you
now know by heart (breakeven, cost/sq ft, revenue share, peak cash need,
expected IRR).

**Week 2 — Account architecture.** Concept: `03-money-management.md`.
*Action:* open/rename the account structure (RERA, operating, reserve,
drawings); move 6 months of any current interest obligations into reserve.
Deliverable: one-line list of accounts + balances, first weekly cash sheet.

**Week 3 — The weekly sheet habit.** Concept: cost-to-complete vs
cash-to-collect. *Action:* compute it for both running sites with your
engineer. Deliverable: the filled sheet; identify any funding gap now.

**Week 4 — Your borrowing file.** Concept: `01-overdraft-and-cc.md` — what
bankers check. *Action:* collect into one folder: 3y ITRs, GST returns,
12m statements, Udyam registration (do it if missing), CIBIL reports for
all 3 partners. Deliverable: gap list — what's missing/weak, with fix dates.

## Phase 2 — Bank money (weeks 5–8)

**Week 5 — NA conversion.** *Action:* shortlist 2 agricultural plots;
meet the local revenue office / consultant; get the conversion cost +
timeline in writing. Deliverable: a one-pager per plot — value if
converted, LAP it could unlock, cost to convert.

**Week 6 — OD shopping.** Concept: turnover method, drawing power,
negotiation checklist. *Action:* meet 3 banks (1 PSU, 1 private, 1 NBFC)
with the Week 4 file; ask each to size a limit. Deliverable: 3 quotes in
a comparison table (rate, fees, covenants, collateral asked).

**Week 7 — Construction finance relationships.** *Action:* meet 2 NBFC/HFC
real-estate desks with the live-deal model; ask what they'd need to
sanction against your NEXT project. Deliverable: their document checklist +
indicative terms, filed for the next deal.

**Week 8 — DSCR + sanction letter literacy.** *Action:* compute DSCR for
your business as-is; take any real sanction letter/offer and mark every
negotiable clause. Deliverable: your DSCR, and the marked-up letter
reviewed with the coach.

## Phase 3 — Private capital (weeks 9–11)

**Week 9 — The investor list.** *Action:* write 10 names who have ₹50L+
idle (family, traders, suppliers, past buyers); rank by trust and money.
Deliverable: the list with per-person likely ticket + preferred structure
(loan vs profit share).

**Week 10 — The raise kit.** *Action:* fill `templates/investor-one-pager.md`
for your next real opportunity; get the LLP name reserved; brief your CA on
`templates/profit-share-term-sheet.md`. Deliverable: printable one-pager +
CA's comments.

**Week 11 — Two live pitches.** *Action:* pitch the two most trusted names
from Week 9 using the meeting script in `04-fundraising-playbook.md`.
Deliverable: written notes — their objections verbatim. (Objections are the
syllabus for week 12.)

## Phase 4 — The system (week 12)

**Week 12 — Partner governance.** *Action:* sit with both partners; fill
`templates/partner-capital-allocation-agreement.md` together; agree
drawings, decision thresholds, deadlock mechanism; send to advocate.
Deliverable: signed instruction to advocate + calendar entry for the first
monthly partner meeting.

## After week 12 — the loop continues

Repeat per new deal: model it (day 1) → raise kit (week 1) → pitch (week
2) → bank take-out lined up before signing the JDA. The coach keeps
teaching one concept per session — pulled from whatever you're actually
facing that week.

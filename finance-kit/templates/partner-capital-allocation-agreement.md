## 2. Profit and loss allocation

2.1 Net profit/loss of each project is allocated **[in capital share
ratio / equally / per Schedule B project-wise ratios]**.

2.2 Where a Partner renders disproportionate effort to a project, the
Partners may resolve in writing, *before that project's launch*, a
**working partner remuneration** of ₹[ ]/month or [ ]% of that project's
profit, charged before profit allocation.

## 3. Drawings and distributions

3.1 Each Partner may draw a fixed **₹[ ]/month** against expected profit.
3.2 Profit distributions occur only: (a) after a project's take-out
financing is repaid, (b) after the interest & tax reserves are fully
funded, and (c) by resolution of Partners holding [75]% of shares.
3.3 No Partner may withdraw, lend, or pledge Firm funds or assets outside
Clauses 3.1–3.2. Violations are recoverable with interest at [18]% p.a.

## 4. Decisions

4.1 **Ordinary decisions** (site operations, purchases ≤ ₹[10] lakh):
any Partner individually.
4.2 **Major decisions** (any of: new project/JV; borrowing; giving any
guarantee; signing any JDA; expenditure > ₹[ ] lakh; hiring/firing key
staff; settling disputes > ₹[ ] lakh): written consent of **at least two**
Partners.
4.3 **Fundamental decisions** (admission/retirement of a partner; change
of profit ratios; sale of Firm property; winding up): **unanimous** written
consent.

## 5. Guarantees and personal risk

5.1 Personal guarantees for Firm borrowings are given by all Partners
jointly, or by none, unless unanimously agreed otherwise.
5.2 A register of all guarantees (lender, amount, project, release
condition) is maintained and reviewed at the monthly meeting.

## 6. Books, banking, and review

6.1 All Firm receipts and payments pass only through Firm bank accounts
per the account structure in Schedule C.
6.2 Accounts are maintained in Tally by [name/firm], reconciled monthly,
and audited annually by [CA firm].
6.3 The Partners meet on the **[first Monday]** of each month; the weekly
cash sheet and project cost-to-complete statements are tabled.

## 7. Exit, death, and deadlock

7.1 **Voluntary exit:** a retiring Partner gives [6] months' notice. Their
account is settled at: capital + accumulated share of profit of
*completed* projects + [ ]% of book value of work-in-progress (valued by
an independent CA), payable over [18] months with interest at [12]% p.a.
A retiring partner remains liable on guarantees signed before exit until
formally released by lenders.
7.2 **Death/incapacity:** the deceased Partner's share is settled with
their nominee ([name each Partner's nominee]) per 7.1, OR the nominee may
be admitted as partner with unanimous consent of surviving Partners.
7.3 **Deadlock** on a Fundamental decision persisting [60] days: referred
first to **[named family elder / mutually trusted mediator]**; failing
resolution in [30] days, any Partner may invoke buy-sell: they name a
per-share price; each other Partner must either buy them out or sell to
them at that price.

## 8. Non-compete and deal flow

8.1 All construction/JV/real-estate development opportunities identified
by any Partner in [district/city] are first offered to the Firm. A Partner
may pursue a declined opportunity personally only with written consent of
the other two.

## 9. Dispute resolution

Disputes are referred to sole arbitration under the Arbitration and
Conciliation Act, 1996; seat: [city]; language: [ ]. Courts at [city] have
exclusive jurisdiction.

# Overdraft & Cash Credit — the cheapest flexible money

## Why OD beats a term loan for construction

A term loan gives you the full amount on day 1 and charges interest on all
of it from day 1 — but construction spending is lumpy. An OD/CC limit is a
sanctioned ceiling you dip into and repay as cash moves; **interest is
charged daily, only on what you've drawn**. For a business where money goes
out in slabs and comes back in collections, this is the correct instrument.

Cost: secured OD typically runs **repo/EBLR + 2–4%** (roughly 9.5–12.5%),
renewed annually. Compare that to 24% private money: every rupee of need
you can shift from private money to OD saves you ~12% a year.

## The flavours that matter to you

| Product | Secured by | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **OD against property (LAP-OD)** | Residential/commercial/NA property | Your core flexible limit. Agricultural land won't qualify — NA-convert first. |
| **Dropline OD** | Property | Limit reduces monthly over tenure (like a term loan + OD hybrid). Banks like it; good if you can't show high turnover yet. |
| **Cash Credit (CC)** | Stock + receivables (hypothecation) | Once your entity shows steady WIP/receivables. Requires monthly stock statements. |
| **OD against FD** | Your own fixed deposits | Instant, ~FD rate +1%. Park idle project money in FDs, take OD against them instead of breaking them. |

## How banks size your limit (know this before you ask)

For MSME working capital, most banks use the **turnover method**: eligible
limit ≈ **20% of projected annual turnover** (they'll fund 20 of the 25%
working-capital gap; you bring 5% margin). Alternative: the working-capital
cycle method — (receivables days + WIP days − supplier credit days) worth
of operating expenses.

Implication: **your entity's declared turnover is your borrowing capacity.**
A firm billing ₹12 cr/yr through its bank account supports a ~₹2.4 cr
limit. Cash dealings that never touch the books buy you nothing at the bank.

## What the banker checks (the underwriting file)

1. **Entity + vintage** — partnership/LLP/Pvt Ltd, ideally 3+ years old. Udyam (MSME) registration: do it, it's free and unlocks schemes.
2. **2–3 years ITRs + audited financials** — showing *profit*. Don't optimize tax to zero and then ask to borrow; banks lend against declared income. Paying some tax is the price of borrowing capacity.
3. **12 months bank statements** — steady credits, zero cheque bounces, no minimum-balance breaches. Route EVERY receipt through the account.
4. **GST returns** — matching the turnover story.
5. **Collateral + margin** — banks lend 50–65% of property value for OD.
6. **CIBIL/credit history** — both personal (750+) and commercial. No settled/written-off accounts.

## Negotiation checklist (banks expect you to negotiate)

- Get 2–3 competing sanction letters; spread compresses 0.5–1.5% when they know you have options.
- Ask for: reduced processing fee (quoted 1%, settle ≤0.5%), no prepayment/non-utilization charges, annual renewal fee waiver.
- PSU banks are cheaper but slower; private banks faster but pricier; NBFCs easiest but most expensive. Use a PSU/private bank for the limit, keep an NBFC relationship as backup speed.
- Never let a limit lapse at renewal — submit renewal papers 60 days early. An expired limit reads as distress.

## 12-month "become bankable" roadmap

1. **Month 0–1:** Entity in place, Udyam registration, dedicated current account, all collections routed through it.
2. **Month 0–3:** NA-conversion application for one or two agricultural plots (this is your future collateral).
3. **Month 1–12:** Clean GST + TDS compliance calendar. Book profit honestly.
4. **Month 3:** Take a small secured loan/OD (even against FD) and service it perfectly — builds the credit file.
5. **Month 9–12:** Approach 3 banks with audited financials + collateral for a real OD limit. Use competing offers.

## Mistakes that kill OD facilities

- **Using OD for the JV deposit slice** (long-term use of short-term money) — the account never swings back to credit, bank flags it as diversion. OD is for the construction spend cycle; the deposit slice is equity/private money.
- Interest debited monthly must be serviced — an OD where interest itself keeps accumulating breaches drawing power and turns the account NPA in 90 days.
- Round-tripping between two of your own accounts to fake turnover — banks' systems catch this pattern.

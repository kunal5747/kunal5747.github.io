# Fundraising Playbook — private capital at ≤ 2%/month

The goal: a repeatable machine that funds the slice banks won't touch —
the JV deposit + pre-approval bridge — and gets refinanced out by cheaper
money the moment approvals exist.

## The structure of every raise

```
Month 0            Month ~9-12               Month ~30-36
│ Private money in │ Construction finance     │ Collections complete
│ (deposit +       │ + customer advances      │ Profit distributed
│  sanction +      │ REPAY private money      │
│  early works)    │ ("the take-out")         │
```

Private money is expensive per year but held for months. ₹3 cr at 2%/month
for 10 months costs ₹60 lakh — against project profits in crores. The
**take-out is what you're really selling** to an investor: a defined exit,
not an open-ended promise.

## Sources, ranked by realism

1. **Business families / HNIs in your own city** — traders, doctors,
   jewellers, agri-merchants sitting on 7% FDs. Your offer of 18–24%
   secured against a visible local project is genuinely attractive.
2. **Your ecosystem** — suppliers, contractors, brokers, past buyers.
   They've watched you build; diligence is already done.
3. **Landowners from previous deals** — they know your execution firsthand.
4. **NBFC real-estate desks** — not for the deposit slice, but as the
   take-out. Cultivate 2–3 relationships before you need them.

## Structure menu (pick per investor)

| Structure | Investor gets | You get | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Profit-share in project LLP/SPV** (preferred) | Capital back first + preferred return + % of profit | No interest clock during delays; regulatorily cleanest | Give information rights, not management rights |
| **Fixed-return loan** (2%/month style) | Monthly/accrued interest + security | Simple, familiar to lenders | Interest runs during delays; TDS @10% u/s 194A; document properly |
| **Flat allotment at deep discount** | Units at pre-launch price | Cash without debt | Only post-RERA; discounts are forever — cap the quantity |

**The waterfall** (put it in writing, it's why people say yes):
1. First collections → investor capital back
2. Then → investor preferred return
3. Then → profit split as agreed

**Security ladder** (offer in this order until they're comfortable):
project-specific LLP so money is traceable → post-dated cheques →
personal guarantee → charge/lien on specific unsold flats → (rarely)
registered mortgage of a property.

## Regulatory red lines (talk to your CA, but know these exist)

- **Raise per project from a small circle of known investors.** Pooling
  money from many/unknown people into a general fund walks into deposit
  regulations (Companies Act s.73/Deposit Rules for companies; RBI's CIS
  and unregulated-deposit-scheme law — BUDS Act 2019). Few investors, per
  deal, documented = normal private business borrowing.
- **A Pvt Ltd cannot take interest-bearing loans from the general public**;
  loans from directors, members (within limits), and other companies are the
  clean paths. **LLPs/partnerships are more flexible** for this — one
  reason to structure project SPVs as LLPs.
- **TDS**: deduct 10% u/s 194A on interest paid to residents, deposit it,
  issue certificates. Investors respect paperwork; it also makes your
  interest cost deductible.
- **State money-lending acts** bind habitual *lenders*; your investors
  lending occasionally are generally outside it — but confirm for your state.
- **RERA**: no collecting >10% from a homebuyer without a registered
  agreement; buyer money is not bridge money.

## The raise kit (what's in your folder when you meet an investor)

1. **Investor one-pager** (template in `templates/`) — the ask, security,
   waterfall, exit month, returns table. One page. Numbers, not adjectives.
2. **Deal model** — the interactive page (`jv-deal-model.html`) showing
   month-by-month cash and the comp next door. Let them drag the delay
   slider themselves; showing the downside honestly closes more money
   than hiding it.
3. **Track record sheet** — the live deal: ₹5.05 cr deployed, structure
   at 4th floor, photographs, approvals. One page per completed/running
   project.
4. **Clean paper** — SPV incorporation, draft agreement (templates here),
   CA contact. Signal: this is a business, not a favour.

## The rate ladder (how 24% becomes 15%)

- **Raise 1:** 24%/profit-equivalent, from people who know you. Deliver
  the take-out ON the promised date even if you must refinance elsewhere
  to do it.
- **Raise 2:** same investors + their referrals, 18–20%. You now have a
  repayment story, not just a project story.
- **Raise 3+:** 15–18%, investors approach you. Start substituting NBFC
  construction finance earlier in each deal.
- Every on-time exit is marketing. Every delay you communicate *early and
  in person* costs you little. Every surprise costs you the ladder.

## Meeting script (the shape that works)

1. The deal in one sentence: *"₹22 cr project, my breakeven is ₹4,070/sq ft,
   the building next door sells at ₹8,300 carpet with no amenities."*
2. The ask: *"₹2 cr for ~12 months to fund the deposit and approvals."*
3. The exit: *"Construction finance is sanctioned-able at month 9; you're
   repaid from the first drawdown, not from flat sales."*
4. The security: waterfall + PDCs + PG + LLP transparency.
5. The honest risk: *"If approvals take 6 months longer, here's the model
   with that delay — I still cover you; here's the reserve that pays your
   interest meanwhile."*
6. Close: *"My CA sends the draft agreement tomorrow; visit the site any
   Tuesday."*
